# Supplied source material

These files preserve the owner's supplied price posters and the original grooming-academy artwork for reference. They are repository source records, outside the web app's `public` directory; the production build does not publish this directory.

- `price1.jpg` through `price4.jpg`: original boarding, dog-bath, dog-grooming and cat-bath posters.
- `groom_academy.jpg`: original academy poster.
- `parsed.json`: literal historical transcription, including ambiguous source rows. It is not the current pricing configuration. Later owner decisions and the reviewed catalog in `packages/pricing/source-rates.json` take precedence; see `docs/booking-and-billing-requirements.md`.
- `groom-academy-original-crop.jpg` and `groom-academy-original-cutout.png`: source-preserving working derivatives.
- `groom-academy-ai-reconstruction-do-not-use.png`: rejected reconstruction retained as an explicitly marked historical artifact. Its reconstructed content is not an approved business fact or a production asset. Use the original poster when reviewing source information.

Do not infer course length, certification, contact details, prices or business rules from a reconstruction or an unresolved poster ambiguity.
